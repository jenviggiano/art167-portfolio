We Create Beautiful, Quality Pieces

Welcome to Elegant Designs! We specialize in CNC machining and custom cabinet making. At Elegant Designs, we are committed to providing exceptional customer service and craftsmanship. We offer a wide range of wood species to suit any design preference. Some examples include:

  • Oak: Extremely popular, durable, with open grain.
    • Red Oak
    • White Oak
  • Maple: Harder than oak, lighter in color, with a fine grain.
    • Hard Maple
    • Soft Maple
  • Walnut: Rich chocolate color, prized for stability and beautiful grain.
    • Black Walnut
    • Claro Walnut
  • Cherry: Develops a rich reddish patina over time, known for superior grain.
    • Black Cherry
    • Wild Cherry
